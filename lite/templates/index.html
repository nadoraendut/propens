3. Settings

How the selection stays fair (giliran)

  • In every Bidang, the quota is filled by the lecturers who have waited longest since they were last audited. Someone audited last semester is not picked again unless the Bidang is too small to avoid it.
  • Lecturers with the same waiting time are chosen by a random draw. The seed is printed so the draw can be shown and repeated.
  • New staff are not rushed to the front: they only count the semesters they were actually in the pool.
  • Auditors must be higher in grade or more senior, and the load is shared evenly, rotating towards people who audited least and were not themselves audited.
