MRSM Alor Gajah · Form 1 · Intake 2026

ALTIUS

Batch shirt design, full sublimation on 100% polyester. Latin for "higher", from the motto Citius, Altius, Fortius. Graduation year 2030. Flat vector mockups, front and back.

Boys

Short sleeve, round neck, regular fit.

Muslimah girls

Long sleeves to the wrist, loose cut, hem below the hips, high round neck for tudung.

Why we designed it this way

Colour palette

Navy, teal and sunrise yellow, plus white. Navy and teal are popular with teens of both genders and hide sweat and dust. Yellow echoes the MRSM crest and gives one bright accent. Boys and girls use the same three colours, so the batch looks united in group photos.

Treatment of ALTIUS

The word is the largest element on the front, in a bold condensed face that reads from far away. It is white on dark navy for the highest contrast, with a yellow underline and the years in teal beneath it.

The "higher" element

Stacked chevrons point upward from the hem and fade as they rise, like a climb toward a peak. The same chevrons repeat on the sleeves. No Olympic rings or logos are used.

Name space on the back

The upper back is plain navy, centred between the shoulder seams and well below the collar. It fits about 15 letters in one line. The dashed box is for guidance only and is not printed. A yellow rule and "ALTIUS 2026–2030" sit under the name.

Muslimah cut and modesty

The girls' pattern is drawn on its own. It has full-length sleeves, a loose straight body, a hem below the hips and a high, narrow neckline that sits under a tudung. Nothing important sits at the neck or cuffs, and the design does not cling to the body.

Sublimation choices

The fabric is 100% polyester, printed edge to edge, including sleeves. All colours are printed as dark ink, so the white lettering is the one exception: it is the fabric colour showing through. Use bright white polyester. The pattern continues past each seam as bleed, and text stays away from seams.

Check before printing

  • Pantone values are approximate. Ask for one printed colour swatch on the real fabric before the full run.
  • Navy is a large solid area. Ask the printer to test for streaks and to confirm the heat press covers the sleeves evenly.
  • Use the high-resolution school logo (vector if possible). The image used here is a low-resolution sample.
  • Confirm the ALTIUS lettering is outlined to curves and nothing sits within 2 cm of a seam or the collar.
  • Confirm the name area is clean on every shirt. Check spelling of all names and the number of letters (maximum 15).
  • Confirm the girls' sleeve length, body width and hem length with a sample, and keep a loose fit when ordering sizes.
  • Fabric must be 100% polyester and white. Cotton blends will fade and the white lettering will print dull.
  • The teacher should approve the logo placement and the use of the MRSM crest with the school before ordering.
